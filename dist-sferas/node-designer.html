Point editor — gestures & keys

The keys below act on the point canvas — click it first: on open the caret sits in the SVG box (so you can paste), and there Del edits text. Any drag, marquee, grip or art replacement focuses the canvas for you; every other control on this window leaves the keys working.

tools
the row above the canvas: Select · Pen (click anchors, drag one to curve it; Enter or a click on the first anchor finishes, Esc cancels) · Draw freehand (the stroke is simplified to editable anchors) · Line · Rect · Ellipse · Add point (click a line or curve — the shape does not move) · Rotate. New shapes land inside the group your selection is in, use the colours in the Fill/Stroke bar, and come out selected so you can nudge or restyle them straight away. Picking a tool turns the point handles on.
colour
the Fill / Stroke swatches restyle every selected shape (and the ⊘ chip means "no paint"); w is the stroke width, applied when you leave the box. With nothing selected they set what the next shape you draw will use. The palette is the one the app's own glyphs are drawn from — for anything else, type it in the SVG box.
rotate
pick Rotate and drag anywhere on the canvas (2+ points selected) — the cross marks the pivot and the readout the angle · ⇧/Shift snaps to 15° · ⌥/Alt+←/→ turns by 1° (⇧ 15°) without leaving Select. Rects, ellipses and text turn by gaining a rotate() of their own — their coordinates cannot express one — so their numbers in the source stay readable.
source
the selected shapes' markup is highlighted in the SVG box, and clicking a line there selects that shape on the canvas · Format (bottom row) rewrites the markup with one element per line, groups indented — the drawing is untouched and ⌘Z/Ctrl Z puts it back
select
drag empty canvas = marquee (⇧/Shift or ⌘/Ctrl add · ⌥/Alt remove · click = deselect) · ⇧/Shift or ⌘/Ctrl+click a point toggles just that point · ⌘A/Ctrl A all · Esc clears, again closes
move
drag any selected point — or anywhere inside the dashed frame — moves the whole selection (a plain click inside the frame deselects) · 0.5 snap, aligns to other points' rows/columns · hold ⇧/Shift while dragging = angle lock 0/30/45/60/90°… · ←↑→↓ nudge 0.5 (⇧/Shift 2; holding a key is ONE undo step)
scale
2+ selected → corner grips scale about the opposite corner (⇧/Shift uniform · ⌥/Alt from the centre); a modifier press inside the frame starts a new band instead of moving
delete
Del/Backspace · deleting a curve's ○ straightens the segment; ○ size handles on circle/ellipse/rect aren't deletable — delete the shape from its ● anchor (centre on a circle/ellipse, top-left on a rect); a sub-shape left with nothing to draw goes too (no handles on invisible art); deleting everything leaves the glyph empty, and ⌘Z/Ctrl Z puts it back
undo
⌘Z/Ctrl Z undo the last point edit or art replacement — a move, scale, nudge, delete, Clear, Built-in, a loaded/uploaded/dropped .svg (20 deep; typing in the SVG box resets it)
keeping
nothing here is kept until Apply — Cancel (or Esc from the SVG box) discards this window's edits, and asks first once you've changed something. ⌘S/Ctrl S here says so rather than saving without them. Built-in reloads this node's built-in FastShaders art where it has any — many glyphs were authored right here and have none, so the button greys out for them.
